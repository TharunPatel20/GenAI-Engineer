### 5. Data Analysis Techniques
- **`head()`**: shows the first 5 rows by default. Pass a number to show more.
- **`value_counts()`**: counts the categories in a column.
  - Loan Status: 411 approved (Y), 187 not approved (N).
  - Married: 388 yes, 210 no.
- **`describe()`**: summary statistics for numeric columns only: count, mean, standard deviation, min, 25%, 50% (median), 75%, and max.
- **Quartiles and IQR**: Q1 is the 25th percentile and Q3 is the 75th. The Interquartile Range (IQR) is Q3 minus Q1.
- **Outliers**: values below the lower bound or above the upper bound are flagged as outliers. The instructor noted that outliers usually hurt model performance and are often removed.
- **Business angle**: a financial institution wants to keep its Non-Performing Assets (NPA) low, so average applicant income is an important check.
